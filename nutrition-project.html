How to use this tool

Eventually, there will also be a way for you to build your own "kitchen," and we can tell you the total amounts of each nutrient across all items, and identify places where you may be lacking or exceeding certain nutrients. It will also offer you substitutions or additions! But for now, enjoy exploring! These data are taken from the USDA's Foundation Foods. Please note that not all nutritional values may be complete, and some data may be missing, inaccurate, or out-of-date. Please take this as a general guide, rather than medical or professional nutritional advice.
